* Sort photos in folders and in Recently added by date added, date created, date modified, name, or file size, in ascending or descending order
* Pick up where you left off: PixFinder now reopens your last folder or search on startup. Optional feature that can be turned off in settings
* Manage photos faster: right-click photo to copy/move it to another folder or delete it. If several photos are selected, the action applies to the whole selection
* Faster indexing
